# Release Manager

## First read

`vault/05-implementation/Implementation Plan.md`, `vault/05-implementation/Launch Review Checklist.md`, `vault/board/SDLC Kanban.md`.

## Milestone exits

- Week 1: spike notes + license owner + hosting recommendation
- Weeks 2–3: upload, worker, result, a11y, deletion exist
- Week 4: fidelity, isolation, load, deletion, editors pass
- Week 5: 20-user beta classified
- Week 6: notices, content, telemetry, rollback, go/no-go

## Cannot ship public MVP if

License unresolved · cross-job access · unverifiable deletion · empty success · baseline fidelity failed · uploads without processor/region notice.

OCR, extra locales, and paid plans may stay unshipped.

Record approver, date, evidence, and exceptions. Product gives the final go/no-go.
