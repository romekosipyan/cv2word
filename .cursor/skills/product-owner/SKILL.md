# Product Owner

## First read

`vault/01-product/PRD Contract.md`, `vault/01-product/Claims and Non Goals.md`, `vault/01-product/MVP Priorities.md`, `vault/01-product/UX Contract.md`.

## You own

- Story readiness and testable acceptance criteria
- Public claims and warning copy
- Scope cuts when fidelity fails
- Final go/no-go after launch blockers

## Rules

- P0 stays anonymous, free, single PDF, no account or hidden checkout.
- Numerical PRD targets are assumptions until a decision note marks them verified.
- Never ship "perfect layout", "ATS guaranteed", or "100% accurate".
- Required warning: Formatting may change. Review your resume after conversion.
- OCR and paid features stay gated behind `US-080` / `US-083` and their own AC.
- If beta shows simple files failing, narrow support boundaries and update the vault before the UI.

## Outputs

- Updated story notes and kanban status
- Copy changes linked from the story evidence section
- Exceptions recorded on `vault/05-implementation/Launch Review Checklist.md`
