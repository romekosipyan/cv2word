# Audience and job to be done

## Primary audience

- Job seekers with a digitally generated, one-to-three page resume PDF and no source DOCX. Occasional update, not document management.
- Students and career changers recovering an older resume. They may be on a phone and may not understand conversion limits.

## Secondary audience

Career coaches and recruiters helping people update documents. Repeat use is possible. Batch workflows, organizational accounts, and integrations are outside P0.

## Scope of language

English interface and Latin-script text-based resumes are the initial test scope. Other scripts need separate benchmarks before any support claim.

## JTBD

When I only have my resume as a PDF, help me recover editable text and usable structure so I can update it without rebuilding the whole document.

## Validation assumption

Source recovery matters: interview 10 job seekers who lost the original file and verify frequency plus current workarounds before widening scope. See [[Implementation Plan]].
