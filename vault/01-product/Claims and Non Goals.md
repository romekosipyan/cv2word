# Claims and non-goals

Agents must not invent, imply, or ship copy that contradicts this note.

## Non-goals

- Pixel-identical output, guaranteed pagination, or guaranteed ATS compatibility.
- Resume writing, job matching, scoring, translations, or automatic changes to facts.
- General office suite, PDF editing, document history, storage library, or bulk API.
- P0 scanned resume conversion, password handling, subscriptions, or mandatory accounts.

## Forbidden public claims

- "Perfect layout", "100% accurate", "ATS guaranteed".
- Unsupported comparative claims against other converters.
- Numerical keyword volumes, willingness to pay, or ranking promises.
- Exact layout preservation as a guarantee.
- OCR availability before [[US-080]] ships and passes its own gates.

## Required public disclosures

- "Formatting may change. Review your resume after conversion."
- Supported files, size/page limits, and privacy summary on the upload surface.
- Quality warnings when font substitution, column-order risk, or scan detection applies.
- Processor identities, processing region, retention policy, and contact route before collecting uploads.

## Planning-assumption policy

Targets such as 99% character recall, 99.5% availability, 30s p95, €2–€5 price tests, and six-week delivery are proposed planning assumptions. Do not present them as verified results in UI, SEO content, or analytics dashboards until a dated [[Decisions MOC]] note says so.

## Evidence policy

A waitlist click is not willingness to pay. A download request is not proof the user edited the resume. Character recall alone is not fidelity.
