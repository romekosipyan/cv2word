# PRD Contract

Originating PRD: `ResumeToWord_PRD.docx` (v1.0, 3 October 2026, proposed). This note is the structured contract agents implement. Numerical targets remain planning assumptions until [[Decisions MOC]] marks them verified.

## Decision and scope

Build a focused converter that recovers an editable resume from a PDF when the Word source is missing. Launch with text-based PDFs, a transparent quality warning, anonymous access, and short-lived file storage. OCR and paid features wait until conversion quality and demand are demonstrated.

## Job to be done

When I only have my resume as a PDF, help me recover editable text and usable structure so I can update it without rebuilding the whole document.

## Goals

- Produce editable paragraphs and useful resume structure for supported PDFs.
- Explain quality limitations before upload and again when review is needed.
- Earn relevant organic traffic through a useful converter and original content.
- Operate a bounded, privacy-conscious service with measured processing costs.

## Launch blockers

Launch is blocked by unresolved licensing, cross-job access, unverifiable deletion, empty outputs marked successful, or failure of baseline [[Fidelity Contract]] gates.
