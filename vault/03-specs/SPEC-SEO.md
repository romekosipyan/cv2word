# SPEC-SEO — Landing and content

Owner role: [[Growth SEO]]. Pages: [[Landing Page]] and [[Content Plan]].

## Keyword hypotheses (unvalidated)

"resume PDF to Word", "convert CV PDF to DOCX", "make PDF resume editable". Do not publish numerical demand claims without a dated source.

## Primary landing page

Descriptive title such as `Resume PDF to Word Converter | ResumeToWord`. One clear H1. Working upload tool above the fold. Truthful support boundaries. Synthetic before/after. Privacy summary. How to edit the result. Use "CV" naturally on the same page to avoid competing near-duplicates.

## Technical SEO

- Crawlable server-rendered text, descriptive metadata, canonical URLs, XML sitemap of public pages, helpful internal links.
- Exclude upload/status/result URLs from sitemap and indexing.
- Correct HTTP status codes. No indexable error or thin parameter pages.
- Structured data only where supported and accurate. Do not imply guaranteed rich results.
- No scaled keyword or city doorway pages ([S4](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies)).
- A separate locale ships only with localized UI, support coverage, native editorial review, and hreflang.

## Core Web Vitals (assumptions)

At p75: LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, CLS ≤0.1 on key landing pages.

## Growth window

Eight-week discovery observation after stable launch, without a ranking promise. Evaluate indexing, relevant impressions, landing-to-upload, and successful downloads.
