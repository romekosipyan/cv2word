# Story template

```yaml
---
type: story

title: Short verb phrase
status: backlog

spec: "[[SPEC-X]]"
assignee_role: backend-developer
plan_week: week-2
estimate: M
depends_on: []
blocks: []
tags:
  - story

---
```
